# Privacy Policy — What Did I Eat

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What Did I Eat is a food photo journal. Here's the short version: everything you put into
this app stays on your phone. I'm not running a server, I don't have a database, and I'm
not collecting your data.

## What the app uses, and why

- **Camera / Photo Library** — to let you attach a picture of your food to an entry. Photos
  you pick or take are copied into the app's own local storage on your device.
- **Location (while using the app)** — to note where a photo was taken, if you allow it.
  This stays on your device, attached to that entry. To show a place name (like "Stockton
  St, San Francisco") instead of raw coordinates, the app asks your phone's built-in
  location service to look up the address, which sends just those coordinates to Apple (on
  iPhone) or Google (on Android). You can turn location off in Settings ("Save where you
  were").

Apart from that address lookup, none of this data ever leaves your device. There's no analytics SDK, no crash reporting
service, no ads, no third-party tracking, and no account or sign-in, so there's nothing
tied to your identity being sent anywhere.

## Data storage & deletion

Entries, comments, and photos are stored locally on your device only. If you delete an
entry or a photo in the app, it's removed from your device. If you delete the app, all of
its data goes with it (nothing is backed up to my servers, because I don't have any).

## Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds sync or backup), I'll update
this policy before that ships.
